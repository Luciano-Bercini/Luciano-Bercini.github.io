▸ ABOUT ME

One dev, many worlds

Hi, I'm Luciano, a full-time indie game developer from Italy. I make games on my own: design, code, balancing and release all pass through my hands.

It started as a hobby in 2019 with Arcadium - Space Shooter, a small arcade shooter for mobile. One game led to the next, and what began as a side project spiraled into a full-time career. The Arcadium series grew into a full roguelite on Steam, and in November 2026 I released Grazed, a skill-based incremental arcade game about getting dangerously close to everything that can kill you.

I care about tight game feel, readable chaos and the kind of "one more run" loop that keeps you up too late. Lately I've been exploring very different territory with a horror game.
